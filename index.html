About Bronzefox

Built on Hard Work & Honesty

Bronzefox Contracting is a locally owned and operated Yukon business, proudly serving Whitehorse and the surrounding communities.

We take pride in every project we undertake, approaching each job with care, precision, and a strong work ethic. Our commitment to quality workmanship, reliability, and attention to detail is at the heart of everything we do.

  • Masterful Craftsmanship

    Finishing work done right the first time — no cut corners, no callbacks.

  • Yukon-Tested Durability

    Materials and methods chosen for freeze-thaw cycles and Northern conditions.

  • Transparent Pricing

    Straight numbers, no hidden fees, and a quote that means what it says.

  • Community-Focused

    A Yukon-owned crew, invested in the neighbourhoods we build in.

  • Tailored Solutions

    Every property is different — plans built around your site, not a template.
